I’m a Senior Applied Scientist and Tech Lead at Oracle with over eight years of experience building large-scale AI systems. My current work focuses on responsible AI—driving efforts around LLM alignment, safety tuning, content moderation, and PII detection to ensure secure and ethical AI deployment across enterprise applications. I'm especially passionate about building culturally inclusive and regionally adaptive AI systems that can generalize across diverse global contexts.

At Oracle, I previously led the development of our internal AI code assistant—a generative productivity tool that delivers intelligent code suggestions, documentation, and review support. It's now integrated into engineering workflows company-wide and is projected to save tens of millions annually in productivity gains. Before that, I worked on Oracle’s vision and document AI stack, where I led model development for image classification, document understanding, and key-value extraction. These systems are now powering automation for industries like finance, HR, and the public sector.

Outside of my applied work, I stay active in the research community. I hold a Master’s degree from New York University, where I was advised by Prof. Ralph Grishman. I’ve served as a reviewer, area chair, and program committee member for conferences such as ICLR, NeurIPS, ACL, NAACL, and KDD. I also mentor early-career researchers through the ACL mentorship program. So far, I’ve authored over 15 research papers and have one granted patent, with others in the pipeline. I’m committed to advancing safe, inclusive, and impactful AI—both in research and in real-world deployment.
