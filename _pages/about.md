I'm a Senior Applied Scientist and Tech Lead at Oracle with 8+ years of experience building large-scale AI systems. My current focus is on responsible AI—leading efforts in LLM alignment, safety tuning, content moderation, and PII detection to ensure secure, ethical AI across enterprise applications. I’m especially interested in building culturally inclusive, regionally adaptive models that generalize globally.

Previously, I led the development of Oracle’s internal AI code assistant, a generative platform that improves developer productivity and is projected to save tens of millions annually. I also played a key role in building Oracle’s vision and document AI stack, leading work on image classification, document understanding, and key-value extraction for automation in sectors like finance, HR, and the public sector.

I hold a Master’s from NYU, where I was advised by Prof. Ralph Grishman. I’ve authored 15+ research papers, hold a granted patent, and regularly serve as a reviewer, area chair, and PC member for ICLR, NeurIPS, ACL, NAACL, and KDD. I also mentor researchers through the ACL mentorship program and remain committed to advancing practical, safe, and inclusive AI.
