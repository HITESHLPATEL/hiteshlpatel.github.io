I am a Senior Applied Scientist and Tech Lead at Oracle, where I build large-scale AI systems that move research into production. My work spans foundation models, multimodal and vision-language AI, multilingual NLP, document understanding, retrieval-augmented and agentic systems, and trustworthy AI. At Oracle, I have contributed to major AI initiatives across enterprise generative AI, code assistance, computer vision, document intelligence, information extraction, AI guardrails, and agent orchestration. These systems support real-world enterprise workflows in areas such as procurement, finance, ERP, document processing, and developer productivity.

My research focuses on building and evaluating AI systems that connect language, vision, retrieval, and enterprise intelligence. I am especially interested in models and benchmarks that generalize across languages, cultures, domains, and deployment settings. I have authored more than 40 research papers and filed over 15 patents, with one patent granted. My work has appeared in leading venues including ACL, EMNLP, NAACL, ICML, ICLR, ICCV, WACV, CIKM, and AACL, with recognitions including a Best Social Impact Paper Award at EMNLP and a Spotlight selection at ICML. I also serve the research community as a reviewer, area chair, program committee member, workshop organizer, and ACL mentor. I received my Master’s degree from New York University, where I was advised by Professor Ralph Grishman.
